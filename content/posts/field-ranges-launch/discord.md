## EN

```
🃏 **Field Ranges (Beta) is live in the app**

See, hand by hand, how the field plays a preflop spot: a 13x13 grid, one cell per hand, with the frequency of each action.

**Example spot**
CO opens, SB 3-bets all-in. The grid shows, for every CO hand, how often the field calls and how often it folds. You see which hands it defends more or less and adjust your 3-bet from there.

**Another contrast**
Facing a non-all-in 3-bet, the BTN folds 56.9% of the time. EP folds 39.0%. Same action, different field by position.

**How to navigate**
- Hand trail: open, 3-bet, 4-bet, all-in
- 6-seat table to pick the positions
- Stack and buy-in filters

**Data:** the fold percentages come from 1.42B decisions; the hand-by-hand grid and the 90 spots (each with at least 2,000 hands with known cards) come from 96.4M hands with known cards. 20bb+, all buy-ins. It is a field estimate, not an exact range.
```
